My Client's Stories

Client 1

Take Elena, for instance, a seasoned free-rider who came to us looking for remote heli-skiing
terrain in the Canadian Rockies that few commercial tours ever touch. We designed a custom
backcountry itinerary complete with private glacier guides, turning her dream of carving
untouched powder into an unforgettable reality.

Client 2

Then there's Marcus, a former competitive snowboarder who wanted to share his lifelong
passion for the slopes with his young family without sacrificing top-tier powder runs.
We curated a tailored alpine trip to Val d'Isère that balanced high-octane off-piste guides for him
with world-class ski school programs for his kids.
